Free Online QR Code Generator

Velquro QR Code Generator lets you quickly create QR codes from website URLs, text, contact information, or other content. No account is required.

How to create a QR code

Enter your text or website URL in the box above and click Generate QR Code. Your QR code will appear instantly. You can then download it as a PNG image.

What can QR codes be used for?

QR codes can be used for websites, menus, contact information, event details, Wi-Fi information, product pages, social profiles, and many other everyday purposes.

Is my information uploaded?

The text you enter is processed in your browser to create the QR code. Velquro does not require an account or file upload for this tool.
