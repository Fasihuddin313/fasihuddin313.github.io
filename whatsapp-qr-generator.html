WhatsApp QR Code Generator

A WhatsApp QR Code makes it easy for people to start a conversation with you without manually typing or saving your phone number.

Enter your WhatsApp number, add an optional message, and generate your QR code. When someone scans the QR code, the WhatsApp chat opens with your message ready to send.

How does it work?

  • Enter your WhatsApp phone number.
  • Add an optional pre-filled message.
  • Click "Generate WhatsApp QR".
  • Download the QR code as a PNG image.
  • Share the QR code on your website, flyer, poster or social media.

WhatsApp Business QR Code

Businesses can use WhatsApp QR codes for customer support, product enquiries, service requests, bookings and sales. A pre-filled message can make it easier for customers to start the conversation.

Important phone number format

WhatsApp click-to-chat links use the full international phone number. The number should include the country code and should not contain spaces, brackets, dashes or the international plus sign in the generated wa.me URL.

Is the message sent automatically?

No. The QR code only opens the WhatsApp conversation with the message pre-filled. The person scanning the QR code still needs to press the Send button.
