Velquro's Duplicate Line Remover is a free online tool that removes repeated lines from text. It keeps the first occurrence of each line and removes later duplicates.

How to remove duplicate lines

Paste your text into the box above and click Remove Duplicates. The tool will process the lines directly in your browser.

Useful for lists and text cleanup

This tool can help clean lists, copied data, keywords, names, URLs and other text where repeated lines need to be removed.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The duplicate removal happens directly in your browser. Your text does not need to be uploaded to a server.
