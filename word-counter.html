Online Word Counter

Velquro's free Word Counter helps you quickly count the number of words, characters and sentences in your text. It can be useful for students, writers, bloggers, content creators and anyone who needs to check text length.

How to use the Word Counter

Simply type or paste your text into the box above. The word, character and sentence counts will update automatically as you type.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The counting is performed directly in your web browser. Your text does not need to be sent to a server for this tool to work.
