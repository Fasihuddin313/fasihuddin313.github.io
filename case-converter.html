Online Case Converter

Velquro's free Case Converter lets you quickly change text between uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case. It is useful for writing, editing, formatting and preparing text for documents.

How to use the Case Converter

Enter or paste your text into the text box and select the desired case format. The text will be converted instantly in your browser.

Is my text uploaded?

No. The conversion happens directly in your browser, so the tool does not need to upload your text to a server.
