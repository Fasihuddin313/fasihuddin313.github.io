What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a measurement calculated from a person's weight and height. It is commonly used as a general screening tool for weight categories.

BMI Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Normal weight
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 or above Obesity

BMI is a general screening measure and does not directly measure body fat or overall health.
