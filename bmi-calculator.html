BMI Calculator

Free BMI calculator. Check your Body Mass Index from height and weight.

About this tool

Enter height in centimetres and weight in kilograms to get your BMI and category. BMI is a general screening number and does not replace advice from a doctor.

Body Mass Index is a quick screening number based on height and weight. Enter your height in centimetres and weight in kilograms and this BMI calculator shows your BMI and the standard adult category. It is a starting point for a conversation about health, not a diagnosis.

How to use it

  1. Enter your height in centimetres.
  2. Enter your weight in kilograms.
  3. Press Calculate.
  4. Read your BMI value and category.

Common uses

People use it to track general weight changes, set fitness goals, fill in health forms or compare their value against standard ranges used for adults.

Tips and limits

BMI does not measure body fat, muscle, age, sex or where weight is carried, so athletes and older adults can get misleading categories. It is designed for adults, not children. If your result worries you or you have health questions, speak to a doctor or qualified health professional.

Privacy and convenience

Your height and weight are processed only in your browser and are never saved or shared. Use the result as one data point alongside other measures such as waist size, activity level and medical advice. Checking at regular intervals, rather than daily, gives a more meaningful picture of trends. This tool provides general information and is not medical advice.

FAQ

What is a normal BMI?

18.5 to 24.9 is the usual normal range for adults.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. It ignores muscle mass and body composition, so treat it as a rough guide.

What are the BMI categories?

Under 18.5 is underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 is normal, 25 to 29.9 is overweight and 30 or above is obese.

Is BMI suitable for children?

No. Children use age and sex specific growth charts.
