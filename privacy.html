Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Velquro handles information when you use our website and online tools.

1. Information Processed by Our Tools

Many Velquro tools are designed to process information directly in your web browser.

For example, text entered into the Word Counter, Case Converter, Duplicate Line Remover and JSON Formatter is processed by the website's browser-based JavaScript.

Images selected for the Image Compressor are also processed within your browser for the compression process.

Whenever a tool works entirely in your browser, the information you enter is not intentionally sent to a Velquro server by that tool.

2. Password Generator

The Password Generator creates passwords using your browser's built-in cryptographic random number functionality.

Generated passwords are displayed in your browser and are not intentionally transmitted to Velquro by the password generator.

3. Cookies

Velquro does not currently require users to create an account to use the tools.

If third-party services are added to the website in the future, those services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

4. Third-Party Services

Velquro may use third-party services in the future to support website functionality, analytics, advertising or other services.

Such third parties may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Advertising

Velquro may display advertisements in the future. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalize advertisements.

When advertising services are enabled, additional information about those services and their privacy practices may be provided on this page.

6. External Websites

Velquro may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website you visit.

7. Children's Privacy

Velquro is intended to provide general-purpose online tools. We do not intentionally request personal information from children through the tools.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the website, tools or services change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Velquro's privacy practices, please contact us through our Contact page .
