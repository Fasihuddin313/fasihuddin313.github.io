Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Velquro. We respect your privacy and aim to provide useful online tools with a simple and transparent experience.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Velquro website and its online tools.

2. Information We Collect

Velquro does not require you to create an account to use the tools available on the website.

Some tools operate directly in your web browser. Information entered into these tools may be processed by the browser in order to provide the requested result.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the tools.

3. Browser-Based Tools

Several Velquro tools are designed to perform their main processing in your browser.

These may include tools such as:

  • Word Counter
  • Case Converter
  • Duplicate Line Remover
  • JSON Formatter & Validator
  • Password Generator
  • Image Compressor
  • Unit Converter
  • Percentage Calculator
  • Age Calculator
  • QR Code Generator

Browser-based processing does not mean that every website component is completely independent of third-party services. For example, some tools may load libraries or resources from external services.

4. Password Generator

The Password Generator is intended to help users create random passwords.

Passwords generated by the tool should be handled carefully. Users are responsible for deciding where and how generated passwords are stored and used.

5. Image Processing

The Image Compressor is designed to process supported images within the browser.

Users should keep their own backup of important original files before using any image-processing tool.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Velquro may use cookies or similar technologies directly or through third-party services.

These technologies may be used for website functionality, analytics, advertising or other legitimate purposes.

If advertising services are used, those services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

7. Advertising

Velquro may display advertisements from third-party advertising providers.

Third-party advertising providers may collect or use information such as browser type, device information, approximate location or interaction with advertisements, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Velquro does not control the privacy practices of third-party advertising providers.

8. Third-Party Services

Some features of the website may rely on third-party libraries, content delivery networks, hosting services, analytics services or other external providers.

These providers may process certain technical information when your browser requests their resources.

Their use of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

9. External Links

Velquro may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of external websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party websites you visit.

10. Children's Privacy

Velquro is intended to provide general-purpose online tools. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the website.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the website and provide a safe browsing experience. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, tools, services or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can contact Velquro through the Contact page.
